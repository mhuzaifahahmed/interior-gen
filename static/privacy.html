Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This is a prototype-stage product. Here's plainly what happens to a photo when you upload it:

What we store

Your uploaded photo and the three generated redesigns are stored so they can be displayed back to you after generation. No account or personal information is required to use this tool.

Third-party AI processing

To generate redesigns, your photo is sent to third-party AI providers (currently Google Gemini for room analysis, and OpenAI for image generation) for processing. These providers process the image to produce the output; we don't control their internal retention policies beyond what they publicly state.

What we don't do

We don't sell your photos or generated images to third parties, and we don't use them for anything beyond producing your redesigns.

Data removal

Since this is a prototype without user accounts, there's currently no self-service deletion flow. If you'd like a photo removed, contact whoever provided you access to this tool.

Changes

This policy may change as the product develops beyond its current prototype stage.

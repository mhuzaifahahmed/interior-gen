## What was built (all committed locally)

- A quality gate: 35-floor test matrix, ratchet baselines, a scorecard (original and tier-aware thresholds),
  a hard-violation histogram, and 1477 passing tests (fast tier ~2.5 min, full suite ~5 min).
- A rules table and planner (plot-size bands, size tiers, yards, bonus rooms, verandah/terrace), flag-gated.
- Circulation: reachability from the entrance, no garage entrance, bathroom door rules, one shared
  door list across PNG, DXF and Concept Layout (tested).
- Rendering fixes: labels, column squares off labels, dimension chains, entrance label, open-area
  rooms drawn unwalled, planner-added rooms marked "+".
- Speed: full suite ~30 min -> ~5 min; planner-on generation 45 s -> 17 s with identical output.
- A design note for an explicit-rectangles packer (`mask-explicit-rectangles-design-note.md`) and a
  working prototype of it, which wins the agreed go/no-go test.

## Honest caveats (say these out loud)

- The live default has NOT improved much for users: the improvements are behind flags that are off.
- The packer's 32/35 is graded by our own scorer. Independent checks: no requested room is dropped
  (0 of 35 floors), stairs are 40-44 sq ft, renders look like real layouts on 40x60 and 60x80.
  But: its corridors are bigger (13.5% of floor vs 9.6% for the mask engine, up to 23%), it has more
  windowless rooms (33 vs 13), and masters often come out under target. Two floors fall back to the
  mask engine. It is slower than the mask engine today.
- Big plots still get a small house (about 2,200 sq ft on 150x150). That is a planner size cap, a product
  decision, not a layout bug.
- The capacity dropdown still shows 0 bedrooms / 0 bathrooms (not fixed).
- 7 of 11 engine fixes tried in Steps 4-5 were reverted because they made other floors worse.
